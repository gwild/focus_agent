## What counts as a focus_agent rule

Per counsel: a focus_agent **rule** = SDD **plus** a fail-closed executable check (compiler or CI/xtask).
SDD + journey/reviewer-reject alone = **aspirational at focus_agent** (Milton may still bind via review).
Do not mark a row DONE until the enforcer exists.

Deferral covers the **focus_agent enforcer wiring only** (owner Elliot, tracking [focus_agent#1](https://github.com/gwild/focus_agent/issues/1)). No contract text is deferred: absolute contracts (fail-loud, no-fallback, no-escape, Gregory-SOT, reviewers-not-SOT, sole-SOT-store) are written in full, with MUST/FORBIDDEN severity unsoftened.
